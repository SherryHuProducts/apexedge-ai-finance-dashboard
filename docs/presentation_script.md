# ApexEdge: 15-Minute CFO and Sales Presentation

**Audience:** CFO and Sales VP  
**Timing:** 12 minutes prepared narrative + 3 minutes questions.  
**Disclaimer:** All operating numbers in the demo are synthetic.

## Slide 1, 1 minute — The business problem

“Imagine a consumer electronics company reporting revenue growth while gross margin declines. Accounting can close the books, and Sales can monitor GMV and ads. But management still needs a common, trusted explanation for profitability.”

## Slide 2, 1.5 minutes — My operating model

“Accounting continues to own the ledger, period cutoff and reconciliations. My analyst layer pulls authorized commercial and finance extracts into a validated metric model. Sales owns actions and reports outcomes. The CFO owns thresholds and capital allocation.”

## Slide 3, 2 minutes — Data reliability

“Before analysis, I check gross-to-net revenue, source ownership and missing data. Independent GL totals can be compared against operational Revenue, COGS and closing inventory. Unverified figures are never displayed as reconciled.”

## Slide 4, 2 minutes — Root causes

“The gross-profit driver bridge reconciles the latest two months across volume, SKU/channel mix, realized net price, product cost, freight and tariff. I can quantify the dollar impact of each driver and distinguish observations from hypotheses.”

## Slide 5, 2 minutes — CFO view

“CFO mode emphasizes contribution profit, margin drivers and inventory risk. It supports decisions about where to allocate resources and which assumptions need further validation.”

## Slide 6, 2 minutes — Sales view

“Sales mode uses the same financial measures but offers SKU/channel performance and promotion economics. If Sales proposes another discount, the tool calculates the minimum incremental unit growth required to preserve contribution profit. It does not pretend to predict demand.”

## Slide 7, 1.5 minutes — AI and governance

“Deterministic code computes the numbers. Optional AI summarizes the validated evidence with explicit consent. The human review flow records owner, decision, expected impact and actual outcome. No AI can post accounting entries or change prices.”

## Slide 8, 1 minute — Implementation roadmap

“First connect read-only ERP and commercial feeds and establish metric ownership. Next validate a two-channel pilot. Then extend cost drivers, permissions, operational monitoring and durable action history.”

## Q&A prompts

- What cost items belong in gross margin versus contribution margin?
- How would you reconcile a marketplace settlement to ERP?
- What would you change when SKU-level freight allocation is unavailable?
- How would you measure whether a TikTok promotion created incremental contribution profit?
- Which approval controls must remain human-owned?

## Closing

“My finance background helps me challenge the accounting and profitability definitions. My computer science background helps me turn that logic into a reusable, testable product. I would start with one decision where faster, trustworthy analysis could materially change the outcome.”
